Hello, and welcome. I'm a South African with a full tank, bottles of whisky and a list of places I always said I'd see "one day". One day turned out to be now.

Some trips I take **solo** — just me, and whatever the road brings. Others are **family affairs**, with children or adults now still asking "are we there yet?".

## What you'll find here

* **Honest write-ups** of where I stayed, ate and wandered — the good and the not-so-good.
* **Real costs in rand**, so you can plan and budget with confidence.
* **Practical tips for travelling at any age** — comfortable shoes, sensible insurance and the best padkos or snacks for a long drive.

Questions about a trip, or a place you think I should see? I read every message. Use the email link at the bottom of any page.
