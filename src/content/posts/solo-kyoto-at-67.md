New York City is a vibrant cosmopolitan city with great diversity and landmarks.  The city was on top of my bucket list, largely due to the movies I had watched while growing up.

**Day 1**

Our couple’s trip to New York City started in Times Square with the Uber Shuttle dropping us at our hotel, **Pods Time Square** on 42nd street on 6th June 2025.   We chose this hotel as it is conveniently situated near the 42 Port Authority bus terminal and sub way.  It was also recommended by a colleague who had visited NYC in 2024.  The room itself was small but cosy with outstanding views of Times Square and within our budget.\
After checking in, we took advantage of the beautiful afternoon and walked around Times Square. We wasted no time in purchasing the 3-day red bus ticket near our hotel.  This hop on and hop off option was ideal for our stay in NYC.
 I observed many tourists walking around with their luggage exactly as we remembered from social media and movies.  When daylight disappeared, the lights from all the billboards made us think that it is was still daytime.  Smoke bellowing from the underground was observed from a few manholes and it was an amazing feeling actually being in NYC.\
The view at night from our hotel was magnificent appearing bright all the time.  We had some New York pizza slices for dinner before retiring to our cosy home for the next week.

**Day 2**

The hotel offered free guided city tours, so our second day started with an exciting four-hour walking tour which included a ride on the subway.  This experience of the sub way was necessary for the remainder of our stay in NYC.   It’s easy to figure out the maps in the subway with the ‘tap and go’ credit card facility enhancing accessibility of usage. 
The tour took us through parts of Times Square, Central Park, Brooklyn Bridge, and Downtown Manhattan.  The famous **Brooklyn Bridge** brought back memories of erstwhile movie scenes. After the eventful tour and engagements, we decided to continue exploring the city on our own.   We took the subway back to Time square and proceeded on the bus tour to the **Statue of Liberty**.   The statue brought a tear to my eye and was simply breathtaking to experience. We walked about on the Island and did some shopping at the various factory outlets.  Most of famous brands have outlets on the Island and some items were reasonably priced.   I was impressed with the patience of the people that use the ferry which was free and very comfortable.   The National Museum of the American Indian also brought back memories of the western movies I watched while growing up.  Entry is free and the museum features many years of indigenous jewellery, crafts, and artwork.   The building itself is huge and a must visit for persons who treasure history.

**Day 3**

We started the day with delicious breakfast at the famous **Carlos bakery** and then a short subway to **Queens** which is the largest borough of NYC. A trip to New York would be incomplete without visiting this most ethnically diverse populous area of the world.  A visit to the Queens Place Mall within walking distance from the subway bears testimony to the diversity of the place.\
After a few hours in Queens we returned to Times square and the evening was well spent touring the 5 floors of **Madame Tussauds** and mingling with the wax figures of celebrities.  My better half **Devena** enjoyed the Bollywood celebrities on show.  We ended the evening with a 4D short film. The 39 dollars entrance was worth the experience.

**Day 4**

**New Jersey** is the closest state to NYC so we took a 45 minutes train journey from Penn station and spent some quality time taking in the sights and sounds of the **Newport Waterfront**.  The views from this end of NYC were equally brilliant. 
Back in NYC we returned to **Staten Island** as we wanted to visit the setting of the 2025 Netflix movie Nonnas.  The movie is based on the real-life story of Joe Scaravella, who opened the famous Staten Island restaurant **Enoteca Maria** staffed entirely by grandmother chefs. The restaurant is still open, and many tourists were visiting and taking pictures of the place.  The restaurant was fully booked and we could not even get a space for a cup of tea and a snack.   We also walked Wall street to see the iconic **New York Stock Exchange** and the famous Charging Bull sculpture that symbolises financial optimism.\
We then proceeded to enjoy the architecture of the **Grand Central Terminal**.  The terminal was a hive of activity with beautiful paintings and plenty of places to eat and drink.

**Day 5**

Our visit to the **UN headquarters** to appreciate the statue of our leader Nelson Mandela was memorable.  Mandela is honoured for his dedication to reconciliation and human rights.  The entire building created a sense of peace that calmed my nervous system.   After a few days without curry we had delicious lunch at Curry and Tandoor restaurant located nearby.
The brief visit to **Central Park** included lakes, forests, monuments and good architecture.  More than a day is required to see all the park's attractions.  The visit to the **9/11 Memorial** and lobby area of **Empire State Building** was somewhat rushed due to large crowds and queues but we were alarmed at the number of retail shops and multiple dining options that were filled.  

**Day 6**
Seven hours before our flight was spent shopping in **“five Below”** a huge departmental store that had well priced items.  We also enjoyed **IT Sugar** the largest candy department store and **Hersheys** chocolate world.

New York City really lived up to our expectations, and we left content with profound memories. There are tons of things to do and experience in New York, and no amount of time is enough to fully experience the city.  I ran out of time to do the day trips to Niagara Falls and Washington DC.
