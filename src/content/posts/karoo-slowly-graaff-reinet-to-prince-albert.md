There's a particular quiet you only find in the Karoo. Not silence, exactly — there's always a windpomp creaking somewhere — but a quiet that makes you drive slower and notice more.

## Day one: Graaff-Reinet

I arrived mid-afternoon with a flask of rooibos and a bag of biltong that didn't survive the Valley of Desolation viewpoint. Go up an hour before sunset; take a jersey.

## What I'd tell a friend

- Fill up at every town. The gaps are longer than they look on the map.
- Pack padkos you actually want to eat. Roosterkoek from a farm stall beats anything in a garage.
- Travelling solo here felt safe and friendly — people stop to chat.
- Planning a park visit? Check gate times on the [SANParks website](https://www.sanparks.org) first.
