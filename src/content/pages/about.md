Hello, and welcome. I'm a retired South African with a full tank, a flask of rooibos and a long list of places I always said I'd see "one day". One day turned out to be now.

Some trips I take **solo** — just me, a good book and whatever the road brings. Others are **family affairs**, with grandchildren in the back seat asking "are we there yet?" before we've left the N3.

## What you'll find here

- **Honest write-ups** of where I stayed, ate and wandered — the good and the not-so-good.
- **Real costs in rand**, so you can plan and budget with confidence.
- **Practical tips for travelling at any age** — comfortable shoes, sensible insurance and the best padkos for a long drive.
