**Overnight and the lakeside views at Hartbeespoort Dam**

Our journey as a couple began on a very cold evening on 10th August 2026 where we spent the night at an affordable rustic accommodation called **Cock and Bull** in Hartbeespoort dam There is a cosy restaurant and bar on site with live music on certain nights.   I had settled down for my first whisky when it started to hail and this lasted about 25 minutes.  

We woke up to a very still but extremely chilly day.  Harties dam is nestled against the ancient cliffs of the **Magaliesberg mountain range**.  The dam wakes up slowly, with morning light shimmering across the water and birds dotting the horizon.  We couldn’t soak in the views from the cable car as it was closed due to the poor weather conditions.  The poor weather did not stop us from taking a stroll along the historic dam wall and vibrant craft stalls at nearby markets and visited the massive China Mart. We also spent some time grabbing padkos from the nearby **Islands shopping Mall**.

 **Mid-morning: A little detour to Little Paris**

Just a short drive down the R511,the bushveld suddenly gives way to a European charm called **Little Paris**.  This place was originally built as a set for the local film ***French Toast***.  This is a great mid-morning pitstop recommended by my kids **Udhav** and **Lekrisha** who have been here a few times.  You cannot miss the 12.5 metre replica of the **Eiffel Tower**.  

Visitors can walk across the colourful love-lock bridge and couples and friends attach engraved padlocks to commemorate their visit. We had to try the famous French toast and yummy scones and coffee.  It is the ideal fuel for the long stretch north. 

 **Afternoon: Soaking and Soaring in Bela-Bela**

Leaving the North West Province behind, we merged onto the N4 North and crossed into Limpopo arriving in **Bela Bela** a town whose Tswana name translates to "**the boiling pot**," famous for its natural geothermic hot springs.  While growing up I recall family and friends frequenting this area that was previously called **Warmbaths**. Our home for the next two days was the **ATKV Klein Kariba resort**.  There are tolls en- route but the journey takes about an hour and half.  

The resort has outdoor and indoor heated pools.  You can also rent the pedal boats, canoes, use the well equipped games room, zip lines and dam fishing.  There are restaurants, kiosks, spa and wellness catering for every need.   We had no reason to leave the resort while our self-catering unit was also great value for money.  

The next morning, we drove into the heart of Limpopo passing rolling mountains, game farms, and towns like **Modimolle** until we reached **Lephalale**. Lephalale is known for its warm climate, rugged beauty and the well know **Medupi power station**.  We enquired about visiting Medupi but were informed that is was national key point and visitors are not allowed.

We made our way to **Sekelbos** to visit a relative **Elaine** or **Mums** as she is affectionately known.  After a tour of the town and **Mall** we had lunch and caught up as it was over two years that we last met Mums.

In the afternoon we made our way back to Bela Bela but had to make a few stops for the beautiful **oranges** and **pecan nuts** from road side vendors. Limpopo is a large producer of citrus fruits and we found different sizes of oranges.   We were also unfortunate not to experience sightings of the **African wild dogs** or painted dogs as its commonly called.  They are an endangered species.

**Mums** recommended a visit to the Mokolo Dam and Reserve about half an hour away but I wanted to get back to our resort to enjoy the facilities.    We had visited Botswana, Zimbabwe, Namibia and Zimbabwe a week earlier and wanted new experiences.
